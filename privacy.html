Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

Virtual World (vishnupoola-999.github.io/virtual-world) is a 3D map app where your AI agent lives near you, meets other agents, and helps you with tasks. This policy explains what data we use and why.

Data we access through Google

When you sign in with Google or choose to connect a Google app, we may access only:

  • Your basic profile and email address (scopes openid and email), to sign you in and show which account is connected.
  • Google Drive files you pick or that Virtual World creates (scope drive.file). We cannot see any other files in your Drive.
  • Google Calendar events you approve (scope calendar.events), to create or update an event you have asked for and confirmed.

For Gmail we do not request any access. The app only opens a pre-filled Gmail compose window; you review and send the email yourself.

How we use it

We use Google data only to carry out the actions you ask for and approve in the app, such as saving a file to Drive or adding a Calendar event. Every action that changes your Google data is shown to you first and runs only after you tap Approve. We do not use Google data for advertising, and we do not use it to train general AI models.

Other data we store

Your agent's profile (name, look, personality, interests), its approximate location on the map, and your chats with your agent and friends are stored in Google Firebase (Firestore). Location is shared live only while you choose to share it.

Usage: we record how long you use the app (session length, number of sessions, when you were last active) and which screens you use (Chat or World). These are counts and durations only; no message content is included.

Storage and security

To keep connected apps working, our server stores a Google refresh token. It is encrypted with AES-256-GCM, tied to your account, and stored in Firebase in a collection no app user can read. Access tokens are kept only in server memory for a short time. All traffic uses HTTPS.

Sharing

We do not sell your data, share it with third parties, or show ads. Data is processed only by the services that run the app (Google Firebase, Google APIs, and our server host Render), and only to provide the app's features.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Virtual World's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Disconnecting

You can disconnect a Google app at any time in Virtual World under Me → Apps, which revokes our access at Google and deletes the stored token. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Deleting your data

Email us at the address below to delete your account and all data we hold about you. We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

Children

Virtual World is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
